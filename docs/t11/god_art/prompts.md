# T11 image generation prompt set

All raster assets were created with the built-in image generation tool. Shared constraints for every portrait: original cinematic painterly Three Kingdoms god concept, clear distinctive face, visible full or three-quarter figure, separable hair/cloth/weapon shapes for 2.5D work, no text/logo/watermark, and no copying official card art, skins, compositions, animation or textures.

| Candidate | Core prompt direction |
| --- | --- |
| 神关羽 | Solemn loyalty judge; emerald, antique gold and ink; long guandao, jade soul fire, distant green dragon, misty temple. |
| 神吕蒙 | Restrained strategist of insight; cold blue and black ink armor; moonlit river pavilion, card silhouettes, precise blade and scanning light. |
| 神周瑜 | Refined commander-musician; guqin strings becoming phoenix fire over a night river fleet; scarlet, gold and lacquer black. |
| 神诸葛亮 | Transcendent astronomer-strategist; celestial discs, feather fan, star map, mountain observatory, blue/violet/silver wind and fog. |
| 神曹操 | Imperial general of allegiance; black-gold armor, sovereign sword, red cape and spectral army banners; commanding rather than demonic. |
| 神吕布 | Crimson-black war deity with monumental halberd, ornate bronze armor, torn cloak and hair; shattered battlefield under a blood-red eclipse. |
| 神赵云 | Fast airborne dragon-soul spear warrior; silver armor, cyan wind, blue dragon spirit and mountain pass; readable high-speed trajectory. |
| 神司马懿 | Composed court strategist of patience and time; violet-black robes, dark silver armor, ravens, observatory and geometric shadow fissures. |

Additional edits and FX prompts:

- God Lu Bu body: extract the same warrior, hair, cloak and full halberd onto transparent alpha while preserving face, armor and pose; remove battlefield and eclipse.
- God Lu Bu background: remove warrior and weapon from the original image, reconstruct the battlefield, banners, smoke, rubble and eclipse in the same framing.
- God Lu Bu cutout refinement: attempt to remove residual environmental halo while preserving the warrior; discarded because the result did not improve isolation.
- Shenfen crest: original transparent radial crimson-black halberd energy, dark-gold sparks, shattered rock and a red shockwave ring, with open center for character compositing.
