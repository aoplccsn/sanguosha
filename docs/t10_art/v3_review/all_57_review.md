## Visual findings

- Zhang Jiao remains the quality benchmark and has not been redrawn. Across the new portraits, character appeal comes from age-appropriate faces, distinctive actions and materials rather than copying his yellow lightning composition.
- Xiao Qiao was redrawn as an energetic young adult at a night lantern festival, with looped braids and freckles. Her old draft resembled Da Qiao's floral hair and coloring; that draft remains in the review directory only. The approved Xiao Qiao has a different pose, expression, light and setting from Da Qiao.
- Fire distinguishes protective Dian Wei, deliberate Xun Yu, weathered Pang Tong, arrow-borrowing Wolong, archer Taishi Ci, coffin-vow Pang De, the two identifiable riders Yan Liang and Wen Chou, and aristocratic Yuan Shao. Wolong references the Standard Zhuge Liang identity while using a different scene.
- Forest's initial Cao Pi draft read as a scholar at a writing desk. In response to the user's correction it was rejected and replaced with the newly enthroned Wei ruler on palace steps holding the imperial seal. Meng Huo, Zhurong, Dong Zhuo and Jia Xu retain the expected age, build and character roles. Zhang Zhaozhang in Mountain depicts two visibly different elder statesmen.
- Mountain includes the mountain crossing of Deng Ai, the troubled last Shu ruler Liu Shan, Jiang Wei's retreat, a youthful conquering Sun Ce, a restrained Daoist Zuo Ci and Cai Wenji's frontier separation. Their quiet or tragic images were judged by narrative and facial expression, not by imitating a battle pose.
- The 57-image overview shows varied camera positions, weather, palettes and locations. Battle images recur where historically apt, but do not share a single background template. At contact-sheet scale no obvious repeated face or markedly unfinished portrait remained. Close individual review found no prominent broken anatomy or duplicate limbs.
